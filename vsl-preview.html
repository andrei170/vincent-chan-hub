VSL Preview Script - Vincent Chan

Built: 2026-05-16 Format: 90-second cohort-application VSL. Shot on Vincent. Embedded into the cohort landing page below the existing flagship YouTube hero. Production note: This is the short cohort-application VSL spec. The longer-form homepage VSL (5-7 minutes) and the course-page preview VSL (4-5 minutes) are scoped separately in the funnel blueprint - both ship Day 4-7 of install. The 90-second cohort VSL ships Day 1-3.

Spec

  • Length: 90 seconds, hard cap at 100
  • Format: Direct-to-camera, talking-head, eye-level framing
  • Setting: Vincent at his existing YouTube production setup. The audience trusts this frame already - it is the same shot they have been watching for years. Do not re-frame for "more professional" or "more corporate." The grounded register reads strongest in the familiar set.
  • Wardrobe: What he wears for normal YouTube long-form. T-shirt, casual button-down, hoodie. Operator-relaxed, not corporate-finance-deck.
  • Lighting: Existing YouTube setup. Soft key + fill. No bare ring-light glare.
  • Audio: Existing YouTube setup. Lav mic or shotgun. Quiet room.
  • Lower-third (on-screen graphic): "Vincent Chan - @VincentChan - NYC, Ex-Wall Street, 3 Years Personal-Finance Creator" appears at 0:03 for 4 seconds.

Script

[0:00 - 0:08] HOOK

If you have watched my YouTube long-form in the last twelve months, you have heard me say I would build a specific operator stack if I were starting over. This is the install version of how I actually run it.

[0:08 - 0:22] CREDENTIAL

Quick context. Economics degree. Left a $120K Wall Street job three years ago to build this brand full-time. ~600K-800K on YouTube, 50,000 on the Sunday newsletter, the $127 Freedom Budget Accelerator is the entry product. This cohort sits above it - the operator-grade install for people whose stack has outgrown what an evergreen course can do.

[0:22 - 0:38] THE GAP

Most personal-finance content lives in two places. Free videos at the top - tips, frameworks, mental models. The Course in the middle - structured budget system, lifetime access. What is missing is the layer where the stack gets personal: budget integration with the portfolio, tax efficiency mapped to your specific income band, retirement vehicle selection at your actual income level, Roth conversion timing, debt strategy, the 12-month operator review. That layer needs live access to someone running the same stack.

[0:38 - 0:55] THE COHORT

I built a 90-day cohort to install that layer. Six to eight operators per cycle. Two-day in-person kickoff in New York hosted by me. Ninety days of weekly cohort calls, office hours, a private cohort room, and 1:1 plan reviews at days 30, 60, and 90. Quarterly cadence. Built for W-2 earners at $150K and above or self-employed operators at $200K and above - that is where the mechanics start earning their weight.

[0:55 - 1:10] OUTCOMES

By Day 30 you have an automated budget system live across your accounts. By Day 60 you have a portfolio allocation mapped against your specific income, timeline, and risk tolerance. By Day 90 you have a tax-efficiency plan drafted and a 12-month execution checklist locked.

[1:10 - 1:25] PRICING + COMPLIANCE

Pricing sits between the free YouTube content I publish weekly and a bespoke 1:1 financial advisor relationship. The cohort teaches the operator stack. It does not guarantee specific returns. Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution.

[1:25 - 1:30] CTA

If you have watched the YouTube, this is the install version. The application is on the page below. Apply, and I will see you on Day 1 of the New York kickoff.

If shooting tight, skip b-roll. Talking-head is enough at this length.

Captioning

Burn-in captions required. Most viewers watch on mobile with sound off in the first 3-5 seconds of the landing-page autoplay. Caption styling: clean sans-serif, white text with subtle drop shadow, positioned in the lower third above any lower-third graphic.

Open and close frames

Open: First frame is Vincent sitting at his existing YouTube production setup, hands visible, mid-sentence. Avoid a wide intro shot or a logo card - the first 1.5 seconds should be voice + face only to hold the autoplay viewer.

Close: Final frame is Vincent holding eye contact at "see you on Day 1 of the New York kickoff." Cut to the page CTA below without a logo bumper. Logo-bumpers cost 2-3 seconds of attention at the moment the viewer is closest to clicking.

Where this VSL lives

  • Primary: Cohort-application VSL on the cohort landing page (landing-page.html), embedded below the flagship YouTube hero
  • Secondary: Pinned YouTube Short on Vincent's channel
  • Tertiary: First IG reel in the cohort launch sequence (with a 6-second cold-open hook + the landing-page URL captioned)

Hero embed on the cohort landing

The cohort landing hero embeds Vincent's verified flagship long-form YouTube video: "If I Started Investing In 2025, This Is What I Would Do" (video ID a0_-xUE12ew, embed URL https://www.youtube.com/embed/a0_-xUE12ew, verified via YouTube oEmbed). The flagship embed warms the cold-traffic visitor with Vincent's existing trust signal before the 90-second cohort-application VSL plays below.

This split (flagship long-form on hero, cohort-application VSL below) is the structural choice. The flagship long-form is already proven - it converts cold traffic to subscribers daily across YouTube. The cohort-application VSL is the bridge from "subscribed audience" to "applicant."

Companion VSLs in the install (not in this doc)

  • Homepage VSL (5-7 minutes) on vincentchan.co - the ex-Wall Street story + brand promise + capture CTA to the 7-Day Money Roadmap. Filmed Day 5-7.
  • Course-page preview VSL (4-5 minutes) on learn.vincentchan.co/fb - what the Course is, six-module walkthrough, who it is for, CTA to the $127 buy. Filmed Day 4-5.

Both are scoped in funnel-blueprint and ship in the same install window. The cohort-application VSL above is the priority install because it gates the highest-revenue tier.

Compliance line in the VSL

The script line "Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution" is non-negotiable. It draws the regulated-niche line for the personal-finance + investment-education category. Do not cut it in editing. The on-screen overlay at 1:18-1:24 reinforces it visually.

The script also explicitly states "It does not guarantee specific returns" - a second compliance anchor that ships in spoken word as well as on-screen text.

What this VSL is not

  • Not a long-form sales letter (the long-form ships after first cohort proves conversion)
  • Not a YouTube-style educational video (the audience is already past the basics; the VSL is a sales asset, not a lesson)
  • Not a story-arc origin video (origin lives in the email sequence and the homepage VSL, not the cohort-landing hero)
  • Not a "what is personal finance" explainer (the audience is past that)
  • Not a feature-bullet pitch deck (the curriculum walk lives in email 3 of the sequence, not the VSL)
