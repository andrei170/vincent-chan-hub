Stage-by-stage detail

Stage 1 - Top of funnel

Surfaces: YouTube, IG, TikTok, Newsletter, Press features, vincentchan.co.

Goal: Pull warm audience attention into one of three converting assets - the Newsletter, the 7-Day Roadmap, or the Course.

Mechanics: YouTube descriptions, IG bio link, TikTok bio, and newsletter footers all route to vincentchan.co (or learn.vincentchan.co/fb for the Course direct-route in retargeting). Single funnel entry per surface. UTM-tagged consistently across surfaces.

Tracking: Plausible or GA4 on vincentchan.co, learn.vincentchan.co, and the cohort landing. Segmented by source.

Stage 2 - Homepage VSL + capture (vincentchan.co)

Format: 5-7 minute talking-head VSL on the hero, above the existing two CTAs ("Send Me The Free Newsletter" / "Yes, I Want the Roadmap").

Content: The ex-Wall Street story (the Entrepreneur feature in his own voice), the brand promise ("Build wealth, buy freedom" anchored), and the single capture CTA to the 7-Day Money Roadmap (the highest-converting current capture).

Tool stack: Direct talking-head shot on Vincent's existing YouTube production setup. No special filming required. Hosted on Vimeo or Wistia for VSL playback (better analytics than YouTube embed for sales-page video). VSL plays autoplay-muted with captions burned in for mobile.

Output: Increased Newsletter + 7-Day Roadmap capture rate. Estimated 20-40% lift on the existing capture flow.

Stage 3 - 7-Day Money Roadmap (free email course)

Status: Already live. No structural change required. The Roadmap is a 7-day automated sequence delivered post-capture.

Re-architecture: add a Day-7 indoctrination email at the end of the Roadmap routing to the $127 Course. Currently the Roadmap terminates with no upgrade prompt - this is the lowest-friction email-rewrite in the install.

Stage 4 - Course sales page (learn.vincentchan.co/fb) - VSL + social proof rebuild

Current state: Long-form text + image blocks. No VSL, no embedded video, no testimonial wall, no named alumni, no press logos rendered with publication names.

Rebuild:

  • Course-page preview VSL (4-5 minutes) above the hero - walkthrough of what the Course is, what the six modules contain, who it is for, and the single CTA to the $127 buy
  • Press wall below the hero - Yahoo Finance, Nasdaq, Entrepreneur, The Tilt rendered with publication name + headline excerpt + outbound link
  • Audience-proof bands rendered cleanly - YouTube ~600K-800K, IG 323K, Newsletter 50K+
  • Named alumni grid (post-install) - 8-12 cards with real first names, real result-anchored quotes, real photos. Collected via post-purchase survey to existing Course alumni in the first 30 days of install.
  • Video testimonials (post-install) - 3-5 short alumni video clips (45-90 seconds each), embedded below the alumni grid.

Output: Estimated 30% lift on existing Course conversion.

Stage 5 - Cohort landing page (NEW)

Surfaces: Single dedicated cohort landing.

Hero element: Embed of the verified flagship YouTube video - "If I Started Investing In 2025, This Is What I Would Do" (video ID a0_-xUE12ew, embed URL https://www.youtube.com/embed/a0_-xUE12ew, oEmbed-verified). The hero embed warms the cold-traffic visitor with Vincent's existing trust signal before the cohort-specific application VSL plays below.

Cohort-application VSL (filmed Day 1-3 of install): 90-second cohort-intro VSL below the hero - the operator-stack pitch + the seat constraint + the application CTA.

Conversion gate: Typeform application (uTtFirS4). Application reads as: current income band, current personal-finance stack maturity (budget / portfolio / tax / retirement), why now, why the cohort. Manual review on submit, approval triggers a Stripe payment link.

Stage 6 - Application + Stripe checkout

Flow:

  1. Applicant submits Typeform
  2. Submission routes to Zapier or Make.com webhook
  3. Webhook fires three actions: - Creates contact record in ConvertKit / Customer.io / GHL (chosen CRM) - Tags contact with cohort-applicant + income band + stack-maturity band - Adds applicant to a #cohort-review Slack channel for manual review (Vincent or his ops contact reviews inside 48 hours)
  4. On approval, applicant receives a Stripe checkout link via email
  5. Stripe purchase triggers: - Cohort welcome email with NYC kickoff date + travel logistics - Add to private cohort room (Circle or Slack) - Add to cohort-active segment in ESP for in-cohort comms

Cadence: 90-day window per cohort. Quarterly cadence (4 cohorts per year).

Stage 8 - Patreon ladder rebuild

Current state: Single tier at £4/mo (~$5 USD), 411 paywalled posts.

Rebuild:

  • Tier 1 ($5/mo): existing tier preserved. 411-post library access. No disruption to existing subscribers.
  • Tier 2 ($25/mo): "Operator Briefing" - monthly long-form deep-dive (10-15 min video + downloadable companion PDF) on one specific personal-finance operator topic (e.g., "The mid-career Roth conversion ladder," "The HSA-as-retirement-vehicle stack," "The 12-month tax-loss harvest cadence"). Quarterly Q&A access with Vincent.
  • Tier 3 ($75/mo): "Operator Inner Circle" - everything in Tiers 1 + 2, plus monthly live group call with Vincent, plus first-look at cohort applications (auto-waitlist for the next Wealth Operator Cohort cycle).

Migration mechanic: Email to existing Patreon base announcing the new tiers + 30-day window to migrate. No forced migration. Existing subscribers default to Tier 1.

Stage 9 - Post-graduation

Cohort alumni:

  • Alumni room (ongoing monthly access)
  • Quarterly cohort-grad refresh call with Vincent (Day-180 / Day-270 / Day-360 / Day-450 from kickoff)
  • Cohort-grad referral program: 10% cohort-credit on referrals that convert
  • Tier 3 Patreon free for life (alumni-only perk)

Integration points (the load-bearing wiring)

  1. Homepage VSL view -> Newsletter / Roadmap capture. No structural change to the existing flow; the VSL lifts conversion on existing CTAs.
  2. Roadmap completion (Day 7) -> Course indoctrination email -> Course-page click -> Stripe checkout. New Day-7 email is the lowest-friction install.
  3. Course-page visit -> VSL view -> press wall view -> alumni grid view -> Stripe checkout. Re-architecture lifts conversion on existing $127 price.
  4. Course purchase -> 5-email warm sequence -> Cohort waitlist tag -> Cohort waitlist nurture sequence (when next cycle opens).
  5. Cohort landing visit -> flagship YT view -> cohort VSL view -> Typeform application -> webhook -> CRM + Slack review channel + status: pending-review.
  6. Manual approval -> Stripe payment link -> Stripe webhook -> cohort onboarding email + NYC kickoff logistics + Circle invite + status: enrolled.
  7. Patreon Tier 3 signup -> auto-tag cohort-waitlist-priority -> next cycle application early-access.
  8. Cohort completion -> alumni status -> ongoing comms segment + quarterly refresh call invite.

Every transition is webhook-driven, every transition is logged, every transition is monitored for failure with email alerts to Vincent's ops contact.

What this funnel is not

  • Not a Course-replacement. The $127 Freedom Budget Accelerator stays in place as the entry tier. The cohort sits above it. The Patreon stays in place at the bottom; only the ladder rebuilds.
  • Not a course-launch model. No 7-day launch sequence, no countdown timer, no $97 evergreen tripwire below the existing $127. Cohort-anchored, quarterly cadence, capped enrolment.
  • Not a paid-ads-first build. Organic warm traffic from the existing ~600K-800K YouTube + 50K newsletter carries the first 2-3 cohort fills. Paid spend joins after the funnel has proven conversion at cold-to-warm rates.
  • Not a robo-advisor or AUM-fee product. The cohort is personal-finance education, not investment advisory. Vincent is not a registered investment adviser; the cohort copy and the compliance footer make that explicit.

Compliance posture across the funnel

Every funnel surface that touches money outcomes carries the same compliance posture:

  • Outcomes vary by capital, market, and operator execution
  • The cohort teaches the operator stack; it does not guarantee specific returns or income
  • The mandatory footer line ships on every surface that touches a paid offer: "Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution."
  • No copy that claims registered-adviser status
  • No copy that promises specific investment returns
  • No copy uses aspirational hook language; voice register guardrails apply (see copy-strategy)

The compliance footer ships on: cohort landing, course-page rebuild, VSL closing card, email-sequence footers, and the Patreon Tier 3 description.
