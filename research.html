Sensitivities (regulated-niche awareness)

  • Personal-finance + investment-education is a regulated-adjacent niche. Any copy that promises specific investment returns, claims registered-adviser status, or implies regulated financial advice is non-compliant. The cohort copy must read as personal-finance education, not investment advisory.
  • Vincent is NOT a registered investment adviser as far as public surfaces show. No SEC registration, no Series 65, no fiduciary credential surfaced. Copy must not imply otherwise.
  • Outcome language is anchored to deliverables, not investment returns. "By Day 30 you have an automated budget system live" reads compliant. "You will save 73% of your income like Vincent" reads as an unverified outcome claim and does not ship.
  • No specific return claims. Cohort copy says "personal-finance operator-grade education." It does not say "X% returns" or "double your portfolio in Y months" anywhere.
  • Voice register guardrails reject aspirational hook language. Vincent's own brand uses "build wealth, buy freedom" - that is grounded and ships fine. Aspirational sales hooks are off-brand for the cohort and trigger regulated-niche flags. The cohort copy uses "operator-grade personal-finance stack" / "automated budget + portfolio + tax stack" / "the 90-day install of the playbook" - concrete deliverables, not aspiration-bait.
  • Mandatory footer on the landing page: "Not investment advice. Results vary by capital, market, and operator execution." Ships in the cohort landing footer, the VSL spec, and the cohort terms. Drawn from the AG1 D100 finance-niche compliance memory.
  • No fabricated alumni or testimonials. The Course sales page renders no named alumni today. The cohort landing for the demo uses copy-block proof structure (Vincent's own credentials, press features by name) and a "stories load on Day 1 of install" placeholder for cohort-grad testimonials.
  • Subscriber-count language uses bands, not exact numbers. "~600K-800K YouTube" reads safer than "exactly 746K" because the count is moving and the audit window changes the headline.
